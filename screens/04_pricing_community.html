What makes winter valley smog so much more hazardous than summer ozone? expand_more

Unlike photochemical summer ozone which dissipates as the sun sets, winter smog is governed by meteorological temperature inversions where a cold pool of air is trapped under a warm cap. Heavy pollutants—including PM2.5, volatile organics, and diesel soot—concentrate near the valley floor for consecutive days without thermal vertical mixing, leading to exponential cellular inflammation and severe respiratory strain.

Do DIY Corsi-Rosenthal boxes actually match expensive HEPA units? expand_more

Yes. Peer-reviewed laboratory benchmarks demonstrate that a standard 4-filter MERV-13 Corsi-Rosenthal box constructed with a 20-inch box fan delivers Clean Air Delivery Rates (CADR) of 400 to 600 CFM. This matches or outperforms commercial HEPA units costing four times as much, due to massive cross-sectional filter surface area and lower air resistance.

How does Aetheris calculate AI route re-routing accuracy? expand_more

Aetheris fuses real-time vehicular OBD-II telemetry, road elevation topographies, and our optical hyper-local sensor mesh. We forecast micro-scale atmospheric settling in freeway cuts and river valleys, rerouting trips along higher ridges or less congested boulevards to lower internal cabin particulate inhalation by up to 64%.
